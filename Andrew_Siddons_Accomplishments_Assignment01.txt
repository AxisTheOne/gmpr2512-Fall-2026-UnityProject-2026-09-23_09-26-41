Accomplishments:
   - Rigidbody2D Ball: Gravity and Reliable Collision.
   - Playfield Boundaries: Left, Right, Top, and Bottom colliders.
   - Death Zone: Ball Respawns after a short delay.
   - Bumpers: Applies impulse and changes color on hit.
   - Bumpers Random Color Change: Bumper has a Random Color bool that enables this.
   - Drop Targets: Drop targets disable when collided, and reset when all are down after a short delay.
   - Portal Gates: Ball is teleported to another portal, preserving velocity.
   - Freeze field: The ball stops for a short time, before unfreezing.
   - Configurable playfield behaviour: 
	- Duration of the Freeze Zone is configurable.
	- Bumper Random Color is a configurable value.
	- Force of a Bumper is configurable.
	- Portals destination Portal is configurable.
   - Scene is organized and objects are set as prefabs to be reused later.
